Performance Management and Development (PMD)
What is PMD?
*Click each button to see the explanation
Select WHAT? or WHY? to see the explanation.
An integrated approach, involving employees and their superiors, to align performance with organizational goals through planning, coaching, evaluation, and ongoing development
01
Ensures that the company’s strategic objectives are translated into employees’ work plans, so every individual contributes directly to organizational success
02
Through coaching, counseling and development plans, PMD supports employees in improving skills and performance overtime
Role of Non-HR Leaders in PMD
Then what is the role of Non-HR Leaders in PMD?
Role of Non-HR Leaders in PMD
*Click each button to see the explanation
Select ROLE 1 or ROLE 2 to see the explanation.
Develop a deep understanding of PMD, as they are directly responsible for guiding and supporting their teams, ensuring PMD success by setting clear expectations, providing coaching and feedback through two-way communication — as the soul of PMD and driving accountability for performance and development.
Work collaboratively with HR to implement PMD processes and ensure they run effectively and consistently across the organization

CONCLUSION

PMD in Employee Experience Journey
Clarity
Through PMD, employees gain clarity in planning the targets they need to achieve and understand how company objectives cascade down to individual goals in an aligned and transparent manner
Motivation
PMD fosters motivation by providing opportunities for development, coaching, and constructive feedback. It also helps employees identify the path required to prepare for career planning opportunities, supported by their superiors.
Engagement
Through PMD approach ensures that employees feel engaged, informed, and empowered to contribute meaningfully to organizational success
